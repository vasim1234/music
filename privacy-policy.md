# Privacy Policy for Bhai Bhai Music

**Last updated:** 21 September 2026

Bhai Bhai Music ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Bhai Bhai Music mobile application.

## Information We Collect

Bhai Bhai Music does **NOT** collect, store, or share any personal information. The app only accesses your device's local storage to display and play your music and video files.

## Permissions Used

- **Storage Permission:** To read and play your local audio and video files.
- **Notification Permission:** To show playback controls on your lock screen.

## Data Storage

All data (favorites, playlists, themes, settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not upload any data to any server.

## Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party services that collect your data.

## Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
